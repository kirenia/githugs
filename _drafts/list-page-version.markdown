<p class="subtitle">everything people have told me to read, watch and play</p>

People keep asking for this, so here it is. Almost none of it is read yet. That's the point of a list.

Most of these came from you. Somebody left a comment saying "you can't talk about this and not read that," and they were right, so it went on the pile. If something is missing, [tell me](mailto:githugs@proton.me) and I'll add it.

<div class="note" markdown="1">
This page changes. I'm not going to keep a tidy "finished" column, but as things get read they turn into videos and posts, and those will link back here.
</div>

## books

### start here

- Neuromancer - William Gibson
- Burning Chrome - William Gibson

  the short story collection. has Johnny Mnemonic and New Rose Hotel in it.
- Snow Crash - Neal Stephenson
- Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? - Philip K. Dick
- Mirrorshades: The Cyberpunk Anthology - ed. Bruce Sterling
- True Names - Vernor Vinge

  1981, so it beat Neuromancer to the punch by three years.
- The Difference Engine - William Gibson and Bruce Sterling

### cyberpunk, deeper in

- Synners - Pat Cadigan
- Islands in the Net - Bruce Sterling
- Dr. Adder - K.W. Jeter
- When Gravity Fails - George Alec Effinger

  also the Budayeen Nights collection.
- Voice of the Whirlwind - Walter Jon Williams
- Market Forces - Richard K. Morgan
- The Peripheral - William Gibson
- The Hacker Crackdown - Bruce Sterling
- GURPS Cyberpunk - Steve Jackson Games

### the ancestors

The ones that got there first.

- The Machine Stops - E.M. Forster

  1909. a novella about people who live alone in pods and talk to each other through screens.
- The Time Machine - H.G. Wells
- 1984 - George Orwell
- Fahrenheit 451 - Ray Bradbury
- Foundation - Isaac Asimov

  plus the robot and empire books if I end up going all the way in.
- I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream - Harlan Ellison
- Shockwave Rider - John Brunner
- The Sheep Look Up - John Brunner

### the blade runner problem

Two different books, and neither one is the movie.

- The Bladerunner - Alan E. Nourse
- Blade Runner (a movie) - William S. Burroughs

### big weird ones

- Seveneves - Neal Stephenson
- Hyperion Cantos - Dan Simmons
- The Player of Games - Iain M. Banks
- Wool - Hugh Howey
- House of Leaves - Mark Z. Danielewski
- BOLO - Keith Laumer
- All Tomorrows - C.M. Kösemen

  the author put it out as a free PDF.
- The Protopias Collection

  starting with POLIS.
- It Takes Death to Reach a Star - Gareth Worthington

### essays and manifestos

Short, free, and most of them are older than they feel.

- [A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace](https://www.eff.org/cyberspace-independence) - John Perry Barlow, 1996
- [A Cypherpunk's Manifesto](https://www.activism.net/cypherpunk/manifesto.html) - Eric Hughes, 1993
- [The Coming Technological Singularity](https://edoras.sdsu.edu/~vinge/misc/singularity.html) - Vernor Vinge, 1993
- "Disneyland with the Death Penalty" - William Gibson, Wired, 1993
- Enshittification - Cory Doctorow

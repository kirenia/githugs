I deleted Spotify recently, and I want to share with you what app I replaced it with and other aopps I'ved switched to in the past two years.

It's important to note that I am not a purist. I'm a solo mom with two kids, a day job, school, a podcast, and did I mention being a solo mom with two kids? I don't have time to self-host my own email, but all the choices I make are downstream from knowing who owns every room I am in and always knowing where the nearest exit is.

| Was                  | Now                           | Why                                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Gmail                | Proton Mail                   | encxrypted, no ads, Tuta is my backup                        |
| iCloud Keychain      | Proton Pass/Authenticator     | open source, works everywhere                                |
| iCloud and Apple One | Proton Drive/physical storage | files go where I put them                                    |
| Chrome               | Brave                         | ..it isn't Google                                            |
| Google search        | Brave Search                  | boring answer but it works fine                              |
| Spotify              | Deezer                        | pays artists and catches AI tracks                           |
| Substack             | My own site + Patreon         | one less platform to ask people to join                      |
| Twitch               | Discord Go Live               | Amazon owns Twitch (ew) my people are already in the Discord |

## My recs

- **Moving your music.** [Tune My Music](https://www.tunemymusic.com) copies your playlists from Spotify or wherever you stream now to wherever you're going. Start it from inside the Deezer app and it's free.
- **Leaving Gmail.** Proton's [Easy Switch](https://proton.me/support/switch-from-gmail-to-proton) pulls in your old email, contacts and calendars, and forwards new Gmail to Proton so nothing gets missed.
- **Your photos.** [Proton Drive](https://proton.me/drive) backs up your iPhone camera roll, Live Photos included. They look like stills in the app and move again once you download them.

## The Spotify thing

It was a few things, first the ICE recruitment ads, then a founder who put his fortune into military drone AI, and more recently a new AI agent pitched as "nostalgia" that in practice means tracking where and when you listen so it can profile you. No thank you, especially not for my kids!!

The podcast stays on Spotify, because the feed comes from my own site and my own storage. Distribution is not endorsement.

## What I still use

**Instagram.** 18,000 people found me there. It's a billboard!!

**YouTube.** The podcast is there through RSS.

**GitHub.** Microsoft owns it and my repos live there for now because that's where people look.

**Apple hardware.** I love my MacBook Pro + iPhone 17 Pro. I locked both down instead of replacing them (especially since I Just purchased both at the beginning of this year and they were quite the investment).

**Discord and Patreon.** Theser are big companies but not exactly big tech. Discord will probably IPO and that's fine, because I could move the community in a weekend if I had to.
